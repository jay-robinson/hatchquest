Support

Help for grown-ups setting up and looking after HatchQuest.

Getting started

The first time HatchQuest opens, it asks to be handed to a grown-up. Tap "I'm a grown-up", pass the gate (hold the button for three seconds, then type the year you were born), and pick your child's dragon name, grade and the month school starts. Hand it back, and your child picks an egg and hatches it by answering a few questions.

The grown-ups page

Tap "Grown-ups" at the top of the home screen and pass the gate. You'll see how your child is doing, topic by topic. You can also change the grade, add another player, turn dragon visits on or off, and see the membership.

Membership

  • The first 100 lanes of Thornkeep are free for good. A membership opens Nova Belt and every world added later, and lets the dragon keep growing past Fledgling.
  • It's $3.99 a month or $24.99 a year in the United States, sold through the App Store.
  • Restore a membership on a new or reset device: open the grown-ups page and tap "Restore purchases".
  • Cancel: on iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then HatchQuest. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Common questions

Is my child's progress on my other devices? Not in this version. Progress is saved on the device the app is installed on.

What happens if I delete the app? All of the app's saves on that device are deleted with it. A membership comes back with "Restore purchases".

Can more than one child play? Yes. Add a player on the grown-ups page. Each child has their own dragon and progress.

Does the app collect anything about my child? No. Read the privacy policy.
